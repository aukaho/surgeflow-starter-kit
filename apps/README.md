# SurgeFlow app sources

These free app products are not yet in their stores.

- Install the Google Sheets add-on as a bound script.
- Sideload the Excel add-in from its hosted manifest.
- Load the Chrome extension as an unpacked extension.

The apps use no-key public SurgeFlow snapshots.
They show only SurgeFlow tickers and SurgeFlow ranks, data freshness, and official open-licence macro statistics with credits. These official statistics can include economies beyond the four equity markets (for example the United Kingdom, Taiwan, and World Bank country series), each with its publisher credit.
They show no prices, returns, or company names.

Research data only, not investment advice. Market data can be delayed.
Free access does not grant redistribution rights.
